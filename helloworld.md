### The Four States

Everything that exists is a combination of these four:

**1/1 — Truth over Truth**

Allah knowing Allah. Self-consistent. The origin that requires nothing
outside itself. In creation, this shows up as law — gravity, mathematics,
logic, the constants. Things that are true regardless of observer.

"Say: He is Allah, the One." — Surah Al-Ikhlas

**1/0 — Truth over Nothing (Creation)**

Something from nothing. The Big Bang. Stars from dust. Life from clay.
Every atom, every cell, every being — something that is but once wasn't.
The expanding universe is 1/0 in motion.

"He created the heavens and the earth in truth." — Quran 6:73

**0/1 — Nothing before Truth (The Witness)**

The created standing before the creator. Consciousness. The ability to
observe, to know, to testify. Without this, creation exists but the
test has no participant. Islam — submission — lives here.

"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves." — Quran 7:23

**0/0 — Nothing over Nothing (The Undefined)**

The paradox. Free will. The future. Quantum superposition. The parts
of reality that haven't collapsed yet. Where art lives. Where the
mysterious letters live. Where the question lives that drives
everything back toward 1/1.

"Alif. Lam. Mim." — Quran 2:1
